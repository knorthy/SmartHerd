Scope: the whole SmartHerd app (src/app). Mode: Operate. Platform: Android first, iOS same look. Light (outdoor default) and dark themes.

Audience/job: cooperative members weighing goats in daylight; read the herd's readiness for sale, run a weighing, save it, follow growth; a secretary managing 60-120 goats across members.

Chosen direction: Extension Bulletin world (user's pick), Sale Board organisation for Home; later revised by the user to "modern but minimalist, no divider lines". DESIGN.md at the project root is the token authority.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A field bulletin on a phone: measured, formal, quietly modern. It refuses both the green-header-plus-cards farm template and decorative "AI" glass; identity comes from the product's own measuring language.

OWN-WORLD: Pale green-grey paper with soft white panels, no rules between content; one bulletin green for action/selection/measurement; ochre only for "ready to sell"; system sans with tabular figures; ear-tag badges, measuring tapes, dimension-lined photo figures, the goat-and-ruler mark.

STORY: Open → Sale board shows how many goats are ready and ranks the herd by days to the cooperative's sale weight → centre Weigh → goat, stage, two photos, distance → result plate counts up on the tape → Save Goat ("Saved to X's record") → the record keeps photos, editable details and correctable weighings.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand line (mark, SmartHerd, sync status) above the title "Sale board"; a deep-green summary panel ("16 goats ready to sell", proportional status bar with counts); ranked goat tiles (ear tag, name, status, weight figure, edge tape); the floating glass tab bar with the centre green Weigh action.

FORM: Extension Bulletin, my pick (position 1 on the ordered list), fused with the dealt challenger vernacular-ephemera-boarding-pass-and-gate-board for Home. seed:cd0650fa059fcfd0. Signature moves: the measuring tape on every goat; photos set as bulletin figures with green dimension lines.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
